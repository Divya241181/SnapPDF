1. Overview

This policy explains what data SnapPDF collects, why we collect it, and the rights you have. We wrote it in plain English on purpose. If anything's unclear, email privacy@snappdf.app.

SnapPDF is a zero-knowledge document tool. Your files are encrypted on your device before they reach our servers. We can't read them. This policy covers the metadata around that — account info, billing, and product analytics.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: email, display name, workspace name
  • Billing: handled by Stripe — we never see your card number
  • Support requests: anything you write us

Information collected automatically

  • Device metadata: device type, OS version, app version
  • Usage events: page views, feature usage — never file contents
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, performance metrics

3. How we use it

We use collected data to:

  • Operate and secure the service
  • Bill you accurately
  • Answer support requests
  • Improve the product (aggregate analytics only)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data, train ML models on your files, or share your usage patterns with advertisers. Ever.

4. Who we share with

We share the minimum data needed with these categories of vendors, under strict data-processing agreements:

  • Infrastructure: AWS, Cloudflare (encrypted blobs only)
  • Payments: Stripe (billing metadata)
  • Analytics: PostHog, self-hosted (aggregate events)
  • Support: Intercom (message contents only)

A complete, up-to-date subprocessor list is at snappdf.app/subprocessors.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

Exercise any right by emailing privacy@snappdf.app. We respond within 30 days.

6. Retention

Encrypted file blobs: retained while your account is active, plus 30 days after cancellation for recovery. Billing records: 7 years per tax law. Support conversations: 3 years. Diagnostics: 90 days.

7. International transfers

Data may be processed in the US, EU, or Singapore depending on your workspace region. All transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

8. Cookies

We use strictly-necessary cookies for auth and preferences. No advertising cookies. No third-party tracking. Full details in our cookie notice.

9. Children

SnapPDF is not directed at anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

We'll email you at least 30 days before any material change to this policy. Version history is public at snappdf.app/legal/history.
